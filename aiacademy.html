This was my first internship after completing my first year of college, and it was truly an honor to experience the AI ecosystem from end to end, from collecting and preparing data to training, evaluating, and improving a model. Before this internship, I had explored different pieces of artificial intelligence through classes and personal projects, but I had never experienced the full machine learning pipeline. During my time at AI Academy Asia, I developed and fine-tuned a Mongolian speech-to-text model using Whisper, Hugging Face, LoRA, and QLoRA, collecting, cleaning, transcribing, and processing over 50 hours of Mongolian audio for training. I worked with voice activity detection and audio preprocessing, benchmarked models using WER and CER, and experimented with different learning rates, training configurations, and hyperparameters on NVIDIA GPUs. Through that process, I improved WER on the Google FLEURS benchmark from 20.2% to 17.4%, a 13.9% relative reduction in transcription errors. But more than the technical results, this internship gave me something I value even more: confidence in growth. It reminded me that if I keep working hard, trust my curiosity, and take one step forward even when I don't know everything yet, growth will follow. This experience made me want to keep going, to seek out more opportunities, build more, and keep challenging myself. Thank you, AI Academy Asia, for giving me that opportunity. This is only one step, and I'm excited for the ones ahead.
